About

Designing with accuracy,

I am Ar. Amit Khandelwal, Founder & CMD of Arch Point Group, an award-winning Jaipur practice I founded with Ar. Poonam Jain. For over 27 years, we have shaped architecture, interior design and PMC across 10+ locations worldwide.

AK

The relationship between vertical energy and the built form decides how a space feels, performs and lasts.

At Arch Point, I design for that relationship, bringing science, Vastu and energy alignment together with design innovation. We have grown from two people to a team of 100+ and delivered 500+ projects across the residential, commercial, hospitality, institutional and civic sectors. As an architect, entrepreneur and author, my goal is value-driven, future-ready design for every client.
